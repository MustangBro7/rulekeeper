three questions, answered from your own logs

Static linters check your files. RuleKeeper checks your agents.

01 · DELIVERY

Did the rule even reach the agent?

Most instruction files silently never make it into context — wrong directory, wrong filename, wrong tool. RuleKeeper detects per-session whether your rules were actually delivered.

02 · EFFECTIVENESS

Did the rule change behavior?

Every rule gets a before/after and an in-context vs not split, dated by the git commit that introduced it. Keep what works. Delete what agents already do by default.

03 · RECEIPTS

Which sessions broke it?

Every violation links to the exact session, timestamp and edit. No vibes — transcript evidence you can open and read.
