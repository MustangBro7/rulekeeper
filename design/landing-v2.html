Method
01 / DELIVERY

Detect what reached context

Per session, per file: did the instructions actually make it into the agent’s context window? Detected from the transcripts already on your disk.

02 / EFFECT

Date every rule by git

Rules are dated by the commit that introduced them. Sessions older than a rule are never accused. Before/after adherence comes free.

03 / RECEIPTS

Attach the evidence

Every verdict links the session, timestamp, command or edit. Open the transcript and check it yourself — the audit is reproducible.
