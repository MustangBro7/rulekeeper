The platform

Three layers, one source of truth: your own transcripts

LAYER 01 — SCAN

Local CLI, free forever

One command reads the agent session logs already on your disk and produces the full context-health report. MIT, offline, zero config.

LAYER 02 — FLEET

Drift across every repo

Near-identical instruction files silently diverge. RuleKeeper diffs the fleet, flags rot vs intentional divergence, and propagates fixes as PRs.

LAYER 03 — SCORE

Continuous effectiveness

Every rule tracked over time: delivery rate, adherence before/after, dead weight to cut. Wired into CI through the GitHub App.
